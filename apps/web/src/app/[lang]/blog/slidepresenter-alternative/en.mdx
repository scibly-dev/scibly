SlidePresenter is a German video platform for employee-generated learning: subject-matter experts record knowledge directly as video instead of building a classic slide deck. It's positioned between a classic authoring tool and a video knowledge base. Like many B2B vendors in this segment, SlidePresenter publishes no public pricing; quotes are put together individually.

## What SlidePresenter does differently from classic authoring tools

| Feature          | SlidePresenter                                                                   | Classic authoring tool (e.g. Storyline)                |
| ---------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------ |
| Starting format  | Video, often recorded directly by subject-matter experts                         | Slides, interactions, often built by an ID team        |
| Effort to create | Low per video, but many individual videos needed                                 | Higher per course, but a more structured overall build |
| Target audience  | Companies with a lot of process/product knowledge held by subject-matter experts | Teams with a dedicated ID or e-learning team           |

## When an alternative to SlidePresenter is worth considering

An alternative is worth considering above all when two things come together: wanting more structure than standalone videos provide, and wanting transparent, plannable pricing instead of a custom quote. Pure video collections with no learning path structure also hit a limit once knowledge needs to be verifiably taught, for example with a knowledge check after the video.

| Alternative                           | Strength                                                                                                  | Best for                                           |

| iSpring Suite                         | PowerPoint-adjacent, shallow learning curve                                                               | Teams that work a lot in PowerPoint                |

## Frequently asked questions

### What does SlidePresenter cost?

SlidePresenter publishes no public price list; quotes are put together individually based on company size and usage scope. A concrete quote requires an inquiry with the vendor.

### Is SlidePresenter a complete LMS?

SlidePresenter is primarily built for video-based knowledge transfer, less for comprehensive learning path management like a classic LMS. Depending on requirements, combining it with a separate LMS can make sense.

### What kind of knowledge is video-based training especially well suited for?

Knowledge that's easy to demonstrate, such as product demos, machine operation, or process workflows, where watching offers real added value over plain text.

### What's the downside of pure video collections with no structure?

Without accompanying structure, such as learning objectives, knowledge checks, and a sensible sequence, it stays unclear whether a watched video was actually understood. Just watching isn't the same as verifiable learning.
