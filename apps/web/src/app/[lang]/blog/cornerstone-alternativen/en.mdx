Cornerstone OnDemand is a comprehensive enterprise system for people development that covers talent management, performance reviews, and succession planning alongside learning management. For many mid-sized companies, that's exactly the core of the problem: Cornerstone is built for a level of complexity and scale that smaller organizations neither need nor can afford. According to market research, implementation costs and ongoing license fees sit well above what a pure training LMS would cost.

## Why companies want to move away from Cornerstone

Three recurring reasons show up in user reports:

- **Feature scope exceeds actual need.** If all you want to do is manage employee training, you end up paying for a system that also brings unused talent management and succession planning capability.
- **High implementation costs.** According to market research, setup costs range from roughly $1,000 for small companies to over $50,000 for large organizations, often tied to external consulting.
- **Long rollout time.** A system of this complexity is rarely deployed productively within a few weeks, which becomes a problem when training needs are urgent.

## What to watch for when switching

Before switching, it's worth doing an honest inventory of which Cornerstone features are actually in use. If you only use the learning management module, you can usually move to a significantly leaner and cheaper system without any real loss of functionality. If you also actively use talent management or succession planning, check whether an alternative covers those functions too, or whether you'll need to combine several specialized tools.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is Cornerstone fundamentally unsuitable for small companies?

Not fundamentally, but the high price and extensive add-on features usually only pay off once a company reaches a certain size with correspondingly complex people development needs.

### Roughly what does Cornerstone OnDemand cost?

There's no public pricing; market research cites a rough range of $6 to $10 per user and an average annual price around $69,000. Actual costs depend heavily on the individual quote.

### Which Cornerstone alternative works for very small teams?

Very small teams do well with systems that offer a free entry tier, such as TalentLMS for up to 5 users, or document-based tools that are ready to use without a long implementation phase.

### Do you have to migrate all existing Cornerstone data when switching?

That depends on how extensively the system was used. Plain course completion histories can usually be exported; more complex talent management data is harder to transfer and should be checked concretely with the new vendor before deciding.
