Scibly embed harness

A stand-in for a customer's website. Serve this from a different origin than the app — a same-origin page proves nothing, because none of the behaviours under test (framing headers, postMessage origin checks, partitioned storage, the referrer that feeds attribution) apply within one origin.

Running it

  1. Start the app: pnpm --filter app dev (port 3001).
  2. Serve this folder on another port: npx serve docs/embed-harness -l 4173
  3. Open http://localhost:4173 and paste an embed URL below, or append ?src=… to this page's URL.

What to check

  • The course renders — a broken frame means the framing header is wrong.
  • The URL above carries no language, and the course renders in the one your browser asks for. Putting /en/ or /de/ back in front of /embed/ pins it to that language instead. Neither form may set a cookie — check the Application panel for this origin after a reload.
  • Before the course arrives you see its skeleton, not a spinner, and the frame does not change height as the real course replaces it.
  • The frame grows to fit the course rather than scrolling internally (that is the script working).
  • The height settles. Watch the log: a few resize messages then silence. A steady stream of ever-smaller heights is F1 — something in the course is sized against the frame instead of against the learner's screen. Check the loading state and both completion screens, which are the tallest such states.
  • Open a lesson. The frame becomes the height from the snippet (600px here) and the player fills it, and it stays at that height as you advance through scenes. A frame that collapses to a strip of top bar and buttons is F2; one that fills the whole window is F4. The change is animated in, and leaving the lesson animates back — the log marks those two messages eased and no others. With the OS set to reduce motion, nothing is marked.
  • Set the frame's height to 480px in the markup above and open a lesson: it still gets 600, because a shorter frame crops the scene rather than tightening it (F7). The overview and completion screens keep sizing themselves to their content, so the resting height is still 480's.
  • On the overview, open a lesson's popover. The frame ends just below the course, with no blank stretch under it and no inner scrollbar. A few hundred pixels of empty frame is F5 — something pinned to the visible band has stopped being position: fixed and is being counted as content.
  • Scenes lay out for the width the frame actually has. A two-column content block stays two columns, and a question's answer zones stay side by side, at any frame wide enough for them — a frame around 700-760px used to fall just short of the desktop breakpoint and stack everything, which doubled the height of a scene and blew its images up to full width.
  • The course reads as a component of this page, not as a phone screen blown up inside it — type, buttons and the footer dock are a step smaller than on the public share link, and a whole question scene fits the snippet's 600px. Open the same course at /public/courses/… to confirm it is unchanged there; the rescale is the embed document's alone.
  • Open a lesson and scroll this page part-way past the frame: overlays stay on the part of the course you can see.
  • Advance a scene while the frame's top is above the viewport — the page should scroll the frame back into view, and should not when the frame is already visible.
  • Remove the <script> tag and reload: the frame keeps its snippet height, the course scrolls inside it, nothing else breaks. This is exactly what an oEmbed consumer renders — the html field is the frame alone — so it is the state a directory judges us in, not the pasted snippet's.
  • Narrow this page to 280px and widen it to 800px: the course stays usable at both ends, with nothing clipped and no horizontal scrollbar inside the frame. Directories reject an embed that only works at desktop widths, and 280 is the floor Embedly names.
  • The session lands in Traffic sources under this page's origin. Serving the harness with Referrer-Policy: no-referrer should instead produce "Embedded (source hidden)".
  • Nothing appears in the app's own analytics for this page's visitors, and no consent banner is shown.
